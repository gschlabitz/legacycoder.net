If you like John Cleese (Monty Python, A Fish Called Wanda) you'll probably enjoy this [Live Vault ad](http://www.backuptrauma.com/video/default2.aspx) starring him as the director of the Institute of Backup Trauma (I almost ended up there, but that's another story). If you like Micheal Dorn (Lt. Worf in Star Treck TNG) you might enjoy it , too. Don't miss the hidden easter eggs: pause the video when the time reads 5:46:618 and, later in the virtual tour of the institute, click the mouse in Room 2 (Diagnosis). Hey! Click the mouse: a visual wordplay... hehe.
