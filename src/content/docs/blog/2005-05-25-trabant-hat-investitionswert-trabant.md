Obwohl die [Trabantwerke in Zwickau](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachsenring_AG) schon lange tot sind, haben die dort hergestellten Fahrzeuge bis in die ferne Zukunft einen hohen Gebrauchtwert. Ich habe mehrere [Trabant](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trabant_%28Pkw%29)en in City 17 sowie auf dem Weg nach Nova Prospekt im futuristischen Endzeitspiel [Half-Life 2](http://hl2.vu-games.com/de/index.php) gesichtet.

![](../../../assets/blog/2005-05-25-trabant-hat-investitionswert-trabant/01.jpg)

Even though the [Trabantwerke in Zwickau](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zwickau#Economy) are long since dead, the vehicles manufatured there have a high resale value far into the future. I have sighted several [Trabant](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trabant)s in City 17, as well as on the way to Nova Prospekt in the futurustic endtime game [Half-Life 2](http://www.half-life.com/).

![](../../../assets/blog/2005-05-25-trabant-hat-investitionswert-trabant/02.jpg)

According to the license plate they all seem to be owned by Russians.

[Wartburg](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wartburg), [Barkas](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barkas) or others didn't surface yet, but once in a while you'll run accross a truck very similar to the [W-50](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/W50_%28LKW%29). All quality products from East-German shores, no West-German cars in the future! Ha!
