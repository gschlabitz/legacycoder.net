**Tips**
- When forming the egg ribbons, stir slowly in one direction with a chopstick while drizzling the beaten egg.
- Sour comes mainly from vinegar, add gradually.
- Primary Heat is white pepper, chili oil/flakes secondary.
- If too sharp, add a pinch of sugar or more stock.
