## Origin

An herbaceous perennial coming from mild biomes with no frost in South America. Blooms all year if it gets enough light. *Semperflorens* means "always flowering."

## Location

Prefers shade, some varieties tolerate full sun. Bring inside before nights go below 50 °F to a bright spot by the window without direct sunlight.

## Watering

Keep soil moist and leaves dry. Monthly liquid balanced fertiliser at half-strength, March to September.

## Pruning

Pinch leggy shoots and spent flowers from April to September. Blooms on new growth, so pinching keeps it compact.

## Repotting

During spring, cut back freely and trim roots. New cuttings go back into the same pot with fresh mix rather than nursing old woody stems. Shallow pots are fine as it roots near the surface.

Can also be rooted in a glass of water (2-3 weeks).
