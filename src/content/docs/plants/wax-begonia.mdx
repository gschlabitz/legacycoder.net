The wax begonia is the workhorse of the genus: glossy, succulent-ish leaves,
flowers from spring until the light runs out, and no drama in between. Its
*semperflorens* name means "always flowering," and indoors on a bright
windowsill it very nearly delivers. It is one of the few bedding plants that
genuinely enjoys pot life — bring it in before frost and it carries on as a
houseplant instead of dying as an annual.

## Care schedule

<PlantCare care={frontmatter.plant.care} />

Stem cuttings root in a glass of water within weeks, so one plant is a
subscription: refresh a tired pot from its own cuttings rather than nursing
old woody stems along.
