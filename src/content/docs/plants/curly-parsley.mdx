Curly parsley is the ruffled cultivar of common parsley, bred for looks as
much as flavor. It is a biennial: leaves the first year, a flower stalk and
seeds the second — by which point the leaves turn bitter, so pot-grown
parsley is usually treated as an annual and resown each spring. It sulks in
summer heat and appreciates a deep pot; the taproot goes further down than
the frilly top suggests.

## Care schedule

Harvest from the outside in — the new growth comes from the center of the
crown, and cutting it there stalls the whole plant.

## In the kitchen

Milder and less grassy than flat-leaf parsley, with a texture that holds up
as a garnish. Chop it into potato dishes, eggs, and butter sauces, or use it
as the base of a classic *Frankfurter Grüne Soße*. Add it at the end of
cooking — heat flattens the flavor quickly.
