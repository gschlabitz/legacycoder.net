## Origin

The ruffled cultivar of common parsley, native to the central and eastern Mediterranean. A biennial: leaves the first year, flowers and seed the second — by then the leaves turn bitter.

## Location

Bright, with a few hours of gentle direct sun, at 59–77 °F. Tolerates light frost but sulks in midsummer heat.

## Watering

Every 2 days; keep the soil evenly moist, never waterlogged. Half-strength balanced liquid feed every 4 weeks, March to October.

## Pruning

Harvest from the outside in — new growth comes from the center of the crown, and cutting into it stalls the plant. A second-year plant bolts no matter what you cut, so a tired parsley is resown, not rescued.

## Repotting

None — resow each spring instead. Give it a deep pot; the taproot goes further down than the frilly top suggests.

## In the kitchen

Milder and less grassy than flat-leaf parsley, with a texture that holds up as a garnish. Chop it into potato dishes, eggs, and butter sauces, or use it as the base of a classic *Frankfurter Grüne Soße*. Add it at the end of cooking — heat flattens the flavor.
