Check the soil not the date. Stretch every rhythm during winter. 

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="🍺 Heavy Drinker">
    Keep evenly moist, not swampy. Mornings and evenings during extreme heat.

    - [Sweet Basil](/plants/sweet-basil/)
    - [Tomatoes](/plants/roma-tomato/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="💧 Moist Critikal">
    Water when surface begins to dry.

    - [Coriander](/plants/coriander/)
    - [Curly Parsley](/plants/curly-parsley/)
    - [Wax Begonia](/plants/wax-begonia/)
    - [Sprenger's Asparagus](/plants/sprengers-asparagus/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="🏕️ Weekend Waterer">
    Wait for top inch to dry, then water deeply.

    - [Christmas Cactus](/plants/christmas-cactus/)
    - [Citronella](/plants/citronella/)
    - [Tradescantia](/plants/tradescantia/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="🌵 Dry-Pot Club">
    Let the pot dry. Soak fully, drain fully.

    - [Lavender](/plants/lavender/)
    - [Rosemary](/plants/rosemary/)
    - [German Thyme](/plants/german-thyme/)
    - [Silver Squill](/plants/silver-squill/)
  </Card>
