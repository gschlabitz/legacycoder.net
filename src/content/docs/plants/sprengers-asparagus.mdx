## Origin

Sold as a fern but a true *Asparagus* — its "needles" are flattened stems doing a leaf's job. From coastal South Africa, mild and frost-free with unreliable rain; the mat of white water-storing tubers is its answer.

## Location

Bright indirect light to gentle morning sun — harsh sun yellows it, deep shade thins it out. No frost. Hang it high: the red berries are mildly toxic and the stems carry small hidden thorns.

## Watering

Moderately moist every few days; the tubers forgive a missed round. Balanced liquid feed at half strength every four weeks, March to September.

## Pruning

Cut yellowed or bald stems out at the base in March. Fresh fronds push from below, not from trimmed tips, so there is no shaping to do.

## Repotting

Every two years in early spring; the tuberous roots bulge or crack a tight pot. Instead of sizing up forever, saw the tuber mass in half and return one half to the same pot — the tubers carry the recovery rations.
