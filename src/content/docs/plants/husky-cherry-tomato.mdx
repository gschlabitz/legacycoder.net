Husky Cherry Red is a dwarf indeterminate — an odd and useful combination.
Indeterminate, so it keeps flowering and fruiting all season rather than in
one flush; dwarf, so it does it on a stocky plant around a meter tall with
short internodes that barely needs staking. That combination is why it is
sold as a patio tomato: it is a cherry tomato engineered for exactly the
container life the Roma merely tolerates.

## Care schedule

<PlantCare care={frontmatter.plant.care} />

The dwarf habit slows sucker growth but doesn't stop it — thin just enough
to keep air moving through the foliage. Cherry fruit splits after a sudden
soak, so steady watering matters more than generous watering.

## In the kitchen

Cherry tomatoes are the pot-to-mouth crop; most of the harvest never reaches
the kitchen. The ones that do are sweeter and thinner-skinned than anything
from a store: halve them raw over pasta with olive oil and basil, or blister
them whole in a screaming-hot pan until they just collapse.
