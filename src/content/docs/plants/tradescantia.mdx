## Origin

A ground cover from the shaded forest floors of southern Mexico and Central America, and it behaves like one in a pot: every stem crawls to the rim and roots at each joint. Pink-silver racing stripes over deep purple, wine-purple underneath.

## Location

Bright indirect light; some gentle sun deepens the purple. The color is a light meter — washed-out leaves mean too little, scorched patches too much. A summer outside in bright shade suits it; bring it in well before cold nights, and hang it out of reach — the sap can irritate skin and pets.

## Watering

Water when the top few centimeters have dried, drier in winter. Half-strength balanced liquid feed every four weeks, April to September — overfeeding fades the stripes.

## Pruning

Pinch long runners back to just above a joint, March to October; the stem branches there, which keeps the pot dense instead of bald in the middle. Every pinched tip roots in water or moist mix within days.

## Repotting

Yearly in early spring — or skip it: when a pot grows tired and leggy, a handful of fresh cuttings planted back into the same pot replaces it outright.
