## Origin

"African" in name only: *Tagetes erecta* comes from Mexico and Central America, where the Aztecs grew it — still the flower of the Día de los Muertos. An annual with upright stems and big pompon blooms; it runs one season and ends at frost.

## Location

Full sun, 59–86 °F. It dies at frost — no overwintering, one season only.

## Watering

Water every couple of days when the surface begins to dry — at the base, not the flowers, since soaked heads rot. Half-strength feed monthly, May to September; overfeeding buys leaves instead of flowers.

## Pruning

Deadheading is the pruning: snip spent blooms before they set seed and new buds keep coming. Pinch the young plant's tip once for a bushier frame.

## Repotting

None — an annual, sown or planted fresh each spring. Seeds saved from spent heads come up easily the next year.
