Sweet basil is a tropical annual and behaves like one: it wants heat, sun,
steady water, and it dies at the first breath of frost. Supermarket basil
pots are dozens of seedlings crammed together to look lush on the shelf —
split the clump into three or four pots and each will outlive the original
by months. Pinch off flower spikes as they appear; once basil sets seed,
leaf production stops and the flavor turns sharp.

## Care schedule

<PlantCare care={frontmatter.plant.care} />

Harvest by pinching whole stem tips just above a leaf pair — each cut
branches into two new stems. Plucking single leaves off the stem is the slow
way to a bare plant.

## In the kitchen

The soul of pesto alla genovese, caprese, and most of the tomato canon.
Basil is at its best raw or barely warmed: tear it over a dish at the end,
since chopping and cooking both blacken it and dull the perfume. Surplus
keeps best blended with oil and frozen — dried basil is a different, sadder
herb.
