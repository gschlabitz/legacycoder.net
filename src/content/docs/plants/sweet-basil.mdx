## Origin

An annual from tropical Asia — warmth, sun, and steady moisture with no cold season. It reads anything below 50 °F as an emergency, has no dormancy, and dies with the summer.

## Location

Full sun in a sheltered, warm spot. Happiest between 59 and 86 °F.

## Watering

Every two days, daily in midsummer — it wilts dramatically and recovers fast. A heavy feeder in a pot: balanced liquid feed every four weeks, April to September.

## Pruning

Pinching is both harvest and pruning: take whole stem tips just above a leaf pair — each cut branches into two — and remove flower spikes on sight, since a basil that sets seed stops making leaves.

## Repotting

An annual, so dividing replaces repotting. Supermarket pots are dozens of seedlings crammed together — split the clump into three or four pots on purchase and each outlives the original by months.

## In the kitchen

The soul of pesto alla genovese, caprese, and most of the tomato canon. Tear it over a dish at the end — chopping and cooking blacken it and dull the perfume. Surplus keeps best blended with oil and frozen; dried basil is a different, sadder herb.
