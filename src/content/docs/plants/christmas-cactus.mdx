Despite the family name, this is no desert plant: *Schlumbergera* grows as
an epiphyte in the moss of Brazilian cloud-forest trees, which is why it
wants filtered light, some humidity, and watering more like a houseplant
than a cactus. It is a short-day plant — the flower buds are triggered by
the long, cool nights of autumn. That is the whole secret to the December
bloom: from October, give it a spot with cool nights (10–15 °C) and no
evening lamplight, then stop moving it once buds show, because it drops
them at any sudden change.

## Care schedule

<PlantCare care={frontmatter.plant.care} />

There is no trimming schedule, but twisting off a segment or two after
flowering keeps it branching — and every segment roots in moist mix, which
is how one plant becomes a windowsill dynasty. Well-kept plants routinely
outlive their owners' other houseplants by decades.
