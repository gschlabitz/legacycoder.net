## Origin

No desert plant despite the family: *Schlumbergera* grows as an epiphyte in the cloud forests of southeastern Brazil's coastal mountains. A short-day plant — long, cool autumn nights trigger the buds.

## Location

Bright indirect light, no midday sun, 50–77 °F. In October give it cool nights (50–59 °F) and no evening lamplight, and stop moving it once buds show — sudden change makes it drop them.

## Watering

Water weekly when the top of the mix is dry, sparser after flowering. Half-strength feed every four weeks, April to September only.

## Pruning

No knife needed: after flowering, twist off a segment or two at the joints to keep it branching. Every segment roots in moist mix.

## Repotting

Every three years at most, in early spring — it blooms best slightly root-bound. Between repots, a fresh top layer of mix stands in.
