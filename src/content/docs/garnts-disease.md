I think I've caught a case of Garnt's Disease. No matter how sloppy and badly produced, 
if a new isekai pops up on Crunchy Roll, I have to watch it.

Half of them have these stupid Shonen power-fantasy fights for every frickin' episode,
but I just have to watch and see what happens. That's the disease ravaging my brain.
